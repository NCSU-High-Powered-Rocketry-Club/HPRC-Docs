## Welcome to High-Power Rocketry!

If you are reading this you must be interested in learning more about rocketry. This is the best place to get started. 

High Power Rocketry involves designing, building, and flying high power rockets. As a club at NC State, HPRC builds rockets for rocketry competitions, traditionally NASA Student Launch. As of the 26-27 school year, the club will compete in IREC instead. However, rocketry is also a hobby that people can just do on their own. As a member of HPRC, we allow you to use our lab space to build your own rocket. We encourage members to do this and to earn certifications because it is fun and is a great experience.

If you are not a member of NC State HPRC, still welcome! I hope that you can find this wiki helpful on your rocketry journey.

## What is a High Power Rocket?

A high power rocket by definition is any rocket flying on a motor with either:  
    A) An impulse greater than 160 Ns.  
    B) An average thrust greater than 100 N.  

However, there is a lot more to high-powered rockets than just this. As touched upon above, there are 3 certification levels which are required for buying motors above certain impulse thresholds. 

## Components of a High Power Rocket

In the very essence, a rocket consists of a motor, airframe, nosecone, and fins. Inside, you would then additionally have some sort of device to slow down the rocket. With larger rockets, this will always be a parachute, while smaller rockets can also utilize a streamer.
